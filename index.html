CORE FEATURES

Everything a woman needs.
In one wearable.

📣
Triple-Tap SOS
Tap 3 times to activate — prevents accidental triggers. Each tap lights up a progress indicator. Activates full emergency mode in under 2 seconds.
📍
Live GPS Tracking
Real-time location update every 2 seconds to contacts and police. Full location trail stored as court-admissible legal evidence.
🎥
Live Audio/Video
Encrypted live stream sent directly to trusted contacts and police. Simultaneously recorded to cloud. Admissible as evidence in court.
🔊
110dB Alarm + Warning Speech
Phone speaker plays a warning message in Hindi or English: "Let the girl go. Your location and video have been sent to police." Loops until emergency ends.
📡
Phone-Free (LTE-M/NB-IoT)
Built-in eSIM connects directly to cloud via cellular. Works even if phone is dead, taken, or out of range. Always-on protection.
🚔
Police API Integration
Direct auto-dispatch to nearest police station with live GPS coordinates. Integrates with state-level emergency systems like Rajasthan Police Shakti app.
HOW IT WORKS

From panic to police in 5 seconds

1

Triple-tap the panic button

Three taps on the band's capacitive button — or say "Help" — activates emergency mode. 3-tap prevents accidental triggers.

2

Band transmits via LTE-M

GPS coordinates, audio/video stream, and SOS signal sent directly to cloud server via cellular. No phone required.

3

Phone plays warning speech

Mobile speaker activates: "Let the girl go. Your location and video have been sent to police..." Loops in Hindi or English until emergency ends.

4

Cloud routes alerts simultaneously

Trusted contacts receive SMS + push notification with live map link. Police dashboard auto-dispatched. All happening in parallel.

5

Help arrives. Evidence secured.

All recordings stored encrypted on cloud — available as legal proof. User presses "I am safe" to close the incident.
